# SteadyStud — Channels strategy (DIY-first, Amazon-avoid)

## Category reality check
Stud finders are a known commodity. To win without Amazon, SteadyStud must be **visibly different** and **easier for average DIYers**:
- differentiation: **find → mark** (notch) + clear “confirm a stud line” method
- reduce returns: instruction card + support content + honest expectations

## Competitive pricing signal (directional)
Commodity magnetic stud finders appear commonly priced around the low teens (e.g., StudBuddy has appeared around ~$10.97 in Home Depot snippets).
Source (snippet-level, confirm with multiple sources): https://www.homedepot.com/p/The-StudBuddy-Magnetic-Stud-Finder-000107/205554450

## Channel decision (maximize long-term profits; avoid Amazon)
### Phase 1 — DTC-first (profit + learning)
**Why it’s best long-term:**
- own the customer relationship (email/SMS, remarketing, repeat purchases)
- better gross margin potential vs marketplaces
- control the education layer (reduces returns and bad reviews)

**Risks:**
- CAC can overwhelm a low-ASP tool unless you create demand (UGC, SEO, partnerships)

### Phase 2 — Retail (highest scale with strong unit economics)
**Why:**
- stud finders are an in-aisle, context-driven purchase
- strong packaging + clear differentiation can win impulse buys

**Best path:**
- regional / independent hardware first (ACE / True Value / local chains)
- then big-box when you can show velocity + low returns + packaging maturity

## DTC tactics that fit DIY
1) **UGC-first creative:** 10–20s “slide → snap → mark → hang”
2) **Education as conversion:** 30–60s “how to confirm a stud line” video
3) **Bundles to raise AOV:** “Hang Kit” (tool + pencil + anchors/screws)
4) **Seasonal timing:** moving season + back-to-school + holidays

## What to test first (low-cost, high-signal)
- Landing page messaging test: “Hang it right the first time” vs “No batteries / no calibration”
- Price test: $14.99 vs $16.99 vs $19.99 (bundle vs standalone)
- Returns-risk test: include quick-start card vs not (expect meaningful reduction)
