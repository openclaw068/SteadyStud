# SteadyStud — Risks, Legal, IP (DIY-first)

## Key product/market risks
1) **Commoditization**: magnetic stud finders already exist.
2) **Expectation mismatch**: magnetic finders detect **fasteners**, not wood directly.
3) **Wall variability**: fastener patterns differ; lath/plaster, metal mesh, etc.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stud_finder

## Safety / compliance considerations
- Loose magnets are a safety hazard (ingestion risk) and can affect medical devices.
- Packaging + insert should include clear warnings.

## Retail-specific risk
Retailers care deeply about:
- **return rate** (especially “didn’t work”)
- packaging clarity
Mitigation: honest claims + quick-start insert + QR tutorial.

## IP / defensibility
- Likely low patent moat on “magnetic stud finder.”
- Best defensible assets:
  - **Trademark** (SteadyStud)
  - **Design patent** on distinctive shape/notch/overall appearance (consider pre-launch)
  - Brand, packaging, and distribution relationships

## Next actions

- Decide on design patent timing before broad public launch
