# SteadyStud — Positioning (DIY-first)

## Positioning statement
**SteadyStud helps everyday DIYers hang things right the first time** — a no-battery magnetic stud finder that *snaps* to hidden fasteners and lets you **mark immediately** with a built-in notch.

## What’s different vs a commodity magnetic stud finder
- The **marking notch** is the hero: find → mark without swapping tools.
- The **DIY confidence method**: simple instructions to confirm a stud line by finding multiple fasteners.
- Retail-ready feel: wall-safe glide + durable finish.

## What we must be careful about (truth-in-advertising)
Magnetic stud finders detect **fasteners**, not wood directly.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stud_finder

So our claims should focus on:
- “find fasteners to locate a stud line”
- “mark your spot immediately”
- “no batteries / no calibration”

Avoid implying:
- deep scanning
- wire detection
- guaranteed stud center without confirmation

## Proof assets to create
- 10–20s demo: slide → snap → mark → hang
- Simple tutorial: find 2–3 points vertically → draw a line → mark center
- Wall-safety: show smooth edges and glide

## Brand tone
- Friendly, confidence-building, practical.
- Visual language: clean DIY, not contractor macho.
