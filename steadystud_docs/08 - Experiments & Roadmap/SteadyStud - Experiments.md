# SteadyStud — Experiments (DIY-first, retail endgame)

## Experiment 1 — DIY usability test (most important)
**Goal:** prove the notch + instructions reduce mistakes and increase confidence.

Protocol:
- 10–15 participants (DIY homeowners/renters)
- Task: mount a small shelf bracket or TV mount pilot holes (safe controlled setup)
- Metrics:
  - time-to-first-confident-mark
  - number of re-scans / second guesses
  - wall mistakes (extra holes)
  - mark error (distance from true stud line)
  - comprehension: can they explain “fasteners not wood” after reading the insert?

## Experiment 2 — Packaging comprehension test (retail critical)
**Goal:** can someone understand the method in 10 seconds from the box?
- Show the box for 10 seconds
- Ask: “How does it work?” “What do you do next?”

## Experiment 3 — Magnet + glide tuning
- Test 2–4 magnet configs and surface treatments
- Criteria: snap feel, hold strength, easy sliding, no wall scuffing

## Experiment 4 — Price + offer test (DTC)
- DTC page tests:
  - $16.99 vs $18.99 vs $19.99
  - standalone vs **Hang Kit** bundle
- Metrics: conversion rate, refund/return rate, support contacts

## Milestones
- v0: working prototype + notch
- v1: production-intent geometry + surface-safe edges
- v2: retail-ready packaging + insert + QR tutorial
- v3: retail pilot launch
