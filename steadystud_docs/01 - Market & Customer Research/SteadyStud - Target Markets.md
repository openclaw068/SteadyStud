# SteadyStud — Target markets (draft v0.2)

## How magnetic stud finders are actually used (implications)
Magnetic stud finders detect **fasteners** in studs, and users often confirm by finding multiple fasteners and inferring stud location and spacing.

Implication: the best target markets are people who:
- need a quick win for basic mounting tasks
- value simplicity (no calibration, no batteries)
- are okay with a “confirm by finding a couple points” workflow

Source: Magnetic stud finder description: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stud_finder

## Candidate target segments (ranked for launch)
### 1) DIY homeowners + renters doing occasional mounting
**Jobs-to-be-done:** hang TV, shelves, curtain rods, frames.
**Why they fit:** want quick, cheap, no-fuss; hate finicky electronic finders.
**Where to reach:** Amazon, big-box retail, TikTok/YouTube DIY content.

### 2) New homeowners / movers (one-time burst of projects)
**Why they fit:** high demand window; will buy a simple tool that “just works.”
**Where:** Amazon, bundles, mover/homeowner checklists.

### 3) Handymen / small service pros (repeat use)
**Why they fit:** value speed + repeatability; can become advocates if it saves time.
**Where:** Amazon, pro-supply, local hardware.

### 4) Contractors
**Why they might not:** already have favorite tools; may prefer higher-end electronic/radar tools.
**How to win:** demonstrate speed + accuracy + “always works” on tricky walls.

## What we need to validate (research questions)
1) Price sensitivity by segment (DIY vs pro)
2) Top pain points with existing magnetic finders (reviews)
3) The “mark center” notch—does it meaningfully reduce frustration/time?

## Next steps (research method)
- Amazon review mining (top listings) to extract recurring pain points and desired features
- 15–25 short interviews (5 minutes) with DIY homeowners and 5–10 with handymen
- Prototype test: measure time-to-first-confident-mark and error rate
