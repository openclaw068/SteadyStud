# SteadyStud — Experiments (draft v0.2)

## Experiment 1 — Prototype user test (most important)
**Goal:** prove the notch + form factor reduces time and increases confidence.

Protocol:
- 10 participants (DIY homeowners/renters)
- Task: find stud + mark center for mounting a small shelf bracket
- Metrics:
  - time-to-first-confident-mark
  - number of “re-scans”
  - mark error (measure distance from true stud center)
  - subjective confidence (1–5)

## Experiment 2 — Magnet tuning
- Test 2–4 magnet configs
- Criteria: snap feel, hold strength, ease of sliding along wall

## Experiment 3 — Pricing + channel smoke test
- DTC landing page with 2 prices (e.g., $12.99 vs $17.99)
- Collect emails; optionally run small ad test

## Experiment 4 — Amazon listing research (review mining)
- Collect top listings and extract:
  - common complaints
  - buyer use-cases
  - pricing bands

## Milestones
- v0: current CAD geometry + working magnet pocket
- v1: refined notch geometry + ergonomics + surface-safe edges
- v2: production-intent material + branding + packaging
