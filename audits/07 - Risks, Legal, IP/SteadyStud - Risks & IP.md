# SteadyStud — Risks, Legal, IP (draft v0.2)

## Key product/market risks
1) **Commoditization**: magnetic stud finders already exist.
2) **Expectation mismatch**: magnetic finders detect fasteners, not wood directly; customers may misunderstand.
3) **Wall variability**: fastener patterns differ; lath/plaster, metal mesh, etc.

Source: Magnetic stud finder mechanism overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stud_finder

## Safety / compliance considerations
- Loose magnets are a safety hazard (ingestion risk) and can affect medical devices.
- Packaging + instructions should include warnings.

## IP / defensibility
- Likely low patent moat on “magnetic stud finder” itself.
- Potential IP angles:
  - **Design patent** on distinctive shape/notch
  - **Trademark** on SteadyStud name + logo
  - Strong brand + distribution + reviews as the real moat

## Next actions
- Prior art scan for: magnetic stud finder + marking notch + specific geometry
- Decide if design patent is worthwhile before public launch
