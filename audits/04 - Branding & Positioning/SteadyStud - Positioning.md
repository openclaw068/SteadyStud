# SteadyStud — Positioning (draft v0.2)

## Positioning statement (draft)
**SteadyStud** is the simplest way to find studs fast — a no-battery magnetic stud finder that *snaps* to fasteners and lets you **mark your spot immediately** with a built-in notch.

## What’s different vs a commodity magnetic stud finder
- The **marking notch** is the hero: find → mark without swapping tools.
- (Potential) improved ergonomics + magnet layout for stronger “snap.”

## What we must be careful about (truth-in-advertising)
Magnetic stud finders detect **fasteners**, not wood directly, so language should be precise unless the product includes a method that reliably infers stud center.

Source: Magnetic stud finder mechanism overview: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stud_finder

## Proof assets to create
- 10–20s demo video: slide → snap → mark
- Side-by-side vs cheap electronic finder (where electronic fails/needs calibration)
- Close-up showing notch marking

- Visual language: tool-like, durable.
