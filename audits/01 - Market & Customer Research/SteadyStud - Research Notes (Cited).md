# SteadyStud — Research notes (cited)

## 1) Category basics: magnetic stud finders
Magnetic stud finders locate studs by detecting **metal fasteners** (nails/screws) used to secure drywall to studs; users typically confirm by finding multiple fasteners and inferring stud location/spacing.

Source: Wikipedia — Stud finder (Magnetic stud finders section): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stud_finder

## 2) What credible reviewers recommend (signals for pro-speed positioning)
### Wirecutter (NYT) — prefers magnetic for reliability
Wirecutter states electronic stud finders are often unreliable and highlights magnetic stud finders as simple and reliable; recommends the C.H. Hanson 03040 as top pick and notes that the pointed end + onboard level simplify marking.

Source: NYT Wirecutter — The Best Stud Finder for Home Use: https://www.nytimes.com/wirecutter/reviews/best-stud-finder/

Implications for SteadyStud:
